Comparison Table
Criteria Sync (REST / gRPC) Async (Kafka / RabbitMQ)
Latency Low — direct response Higher — via broker
Coupling High — A depends directly on B Low — A is unaware of B
Failure Mode Cascading — failures spread Isolated — failure contained at B
Throughput Limited by B's processing speed Buffered by broker — independent scaling
Complexity Simple — clear request/response More complex (idempotency, ordering)
